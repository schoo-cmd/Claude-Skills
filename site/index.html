Our approach

A firm built around a single question — is this business worth owning for a decade?

We invest with the discipline of an owner, not an allocator. Our capital is concentrated by design, our diligence is direct, and our engagement with management is measured and honest.

Heritage Park backs businesses where quality is not accidental — those with structural advantages, durable customer relationships, and leadership teams who take the long view. We are unhurried in our search and deliberate in our commitments.

What we bring

Three things we hold to.

01

Aligned capital.

We invest our own money alongside our partners. Our timelines are measured in cycles, not fund clocks — and our incentives are structured to reflect it.

02

Operator-first partnership.

We back leaders and get out of their way. Our role is to underwrite, to counsel, and to unlock — not to manage from the passenger seat.

03

A narrow aperture.

We look at few deals and pass on most of them. When we do invest, it is because the business meets a high bar we won't lower for pace.

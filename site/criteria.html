Transaction criteria

What we look for.

A concise view of the businesses and situations we are best positioned to invest in. If you are exploring a transaction and think there may be a fit, we would welcome the conversation.

Investment size
$25M – $150M of equity per transaction, with capacity for larger investments through co-investment relationships.
Company profile
$10M – $50M EBITDA · Consistently profitable · Established market position · Recurring or highly repeat revenue · Founder, family, or management-led businesses at an inflection point.
Sectors of focus
  • Industrial services
  • Specialty manufacturing
  • Business & compliance services
  • Healthcare services
  • Value-added distribution
  • Specialty consumer
Geography
Headquartered in the United States or Canada, with a preference for businesses serving domestic markets.
Transaction types
  • Founder & family recapitalizations
  • Management buyouts
  • Corporate carve-outs
  • Growth equity in profitable businesses
  • Take-privates and going-private transactions
Ownership posture
Control or significant minority. We are comfortable rolling equity with founders and management, and we structure incentives to align around long-term value creation.
Hold period
Ten years or longer. We are not clock-driven, and we do not underwrite to a preset exit date.
What we don't do
Early-stage venture · Unprofitable businesses · Turnarounds · Highly cyclical commodity businesses · Businesses without defensible customer or supplier relationships.
A note to intermediaries

We appreciate the work of investment bankers, advisors, and business brokers, and we are direct in our feedback. When a situation is a fit, we move quickly. When it isn't, we say so and explain why.
